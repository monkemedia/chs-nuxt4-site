# SOP: Adding Recent Work and Google Reviews to the website

**Who this is for:** CHS staff who update the website.
**What it covers:** adding jobs to "Recent work", adding Google reviews and testimonials, and updating the Google rating.
**Time needed:** about 10 minutes per job, 2 minutes per review.

---

## ⚠️ The rules (read these first)

These are legal requirements (UK consumer law and Google's policies), not just house style.

| ✅ Always                                                             | ❌ Never                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Add only **real** jobs CHS has done                                   | Make up, exaggerate or "tidy up" a job                                       |
| Use **our own photos** of the job                                     | Use stock photos or photos of someone else's work                            |
| Copy reviews **word for word**, including spelling mistakes           | Edit, shorten, correct or combine reviews                                    |
| Use the reviewer's name **exactly** as Google shows it                | Write a review yourself, or ask friends or family to                         |
| Get the customer's **permission** before naming them or their company | Show number plates, faces or customer paperwork in photos without permission |

If you're unsure whether something is OK to publish, **don't publish it**. Save it as a draft and ask.

## Previewing before it goes live

Want to check how something looks before customers see it? Use the **preview site**: **https://staging.chshydraulics.co.uk**

1. Fill in the entry as normal, but **don't click Publish**. Your changes save automatically as a **draft**.
2. Wait **2–3 minutes**, then open **https://staging.chshydraulics.co.uk** and find your entry. Drafts are marked with a **Draft** badge.
3. Happy with it? Go back to the admin area and click **Publish** to put it on the live website.

> The preview site is for staff only. Don't share the link with customers. Google doesn't list it, so it won't affect the live website.

## 2. Adding a job to "Recent work"

### Before you start

- [ ] It's a **real, finished job**
- [ ] You have a **photo** (optional, but jobs look much better with one): landscape, clear, our own
- [ ] If you want to name the customer, you have their **permission**. Otherwise, describe them (e.g. "a dairy farm near Carmarthen")

### Steps

1. Click **Recent work** in the left menu.
2. At the top of the Recent work list, click the **pencil icon** ("Create new document"). An empty form opens on the right.
3. Fill in the form (see the field guide below).
4. Next to **Web address**, click **Generate**. It's made from the English title.
5. Click **Publish** (bottom right).
6. Wait **2–3 minutes**, then check the job on the website under **Recent work**.

> "No documents of this type" just means the list is empty. It isn't an error.

### Field guide

| Field                           | What to put                                                                           | Example                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Web address**                 | Click **Generate**. **Don't change it after publishing**: old links would break.      | `jcb-3cx-boom-ram-rebuild`                                                                              |
| **Date finished**               | The day the job was completed                                                         | 12/09/2026                                                                                              |
| **Service**                     | The service that best fits the job                                                    | Ram Repairs                                                                                             |
| **Photo**                       | Upload our photo. Drag the circle to the most important part so it isn't cropped out. |                                                                                                         |
| **English → Title**             | What was done, to what. Under 45 characters.                                          | Boom ram rebuilt for a JCB 3CX                                                                          |
| **English → Summary**           | One or two sentences: the problem and the result. Under 160 characters.               | A leaking boom ram was stripped, resealed and back on site within two days.                             |
| **English → Machine**           | Optional. Make and model.                                                             | JCB 3CX backhoe loader                                                                                  |
| **English → Location**          | Optional. Town or area only.                                                          | Llanelli                                                                                                |
| **English → The problem**       | What the customer noticed and what we found.                                          | The boom was drifting down under load and oil was weeping past the rod seal.                            |
| **English → What we did**       | One step per line. Click **Add item** for each step.                                  | Stripped and inspected the ram · Replaced the seal kit · Polished the rod · Rebuilt and pressure tested |
| **English → The result**        | How it turned out: time taken, how the machine runs now.                              | Back at work the same week, no drift.                                                                   |
| **English → Photo description** | What the photo shows, for people using screen readers.                                | Rebuilt boom ram on the workshop bench                                                                  |
| **Welsh (Cymraeg)**             | Optional: see section 5.                                                              |                                                                                                         |

### Writing tips

- Write plainly, like you'd explain it to the customer on the phone.
- Be specific: "back on site in two days" beats "quick turnaround".
- Don't promise things in a job write-up ("we always…", "guaranteed…").

## 3. Adding a Google review

1. Open our **Google Business Profile** and find the review.
2. In the admin area, click **Reviews**, then the **pencil icon** ("Create new document").
3. Fill in:

| Field           | What to put                                                            |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Type**        | **Google review**                                                      |
| **Name**        | Exactly as shown on Google                                             |
| **Stars (1–5)** | The reviewer's star rating                                             |
| **Review**      | **Copy and paste** the text. Don't retype it, and don't change a word. |
| **Date**        | The date on the review                                                 |

4. Click **Publish**.

> We don't have to add every review, but we **must not** pick only the good ones in a way that misleads. If in doubt, add it.

### Adding a testimonial (sent to us directly, not on Google)

Same steps, but:

- **Type:** **Testimonial (sent to us directly)**
- **Company or role:** e.g. "Site manager, Jones Groundworks"
- **Stars:** leave empty unless the customer gave a rating
- ✅ Keep the customer's email or message that gives permission to publish it

---

## 4. Updating the Google rating

Do this **once a month**, or whenever a new Google review comes in.

1. Click **Google rating** in the left menu.
2. Copy the **overall rating** (e.g. 4.8) and **number of reviews** from our Google Business Profile.
3. Click **Publish**.

> The stars under the homepage buttons only appear once we have **10+ reviews averaging 4.5 or more**. The reviews section further down the page always shows.

## 5. Welsh versions

- Welsh is **optional**. Without it, the Welsh website shows the English with a note saying it's only available in English.
- To add Welsh, send the English text to **[translator / contact]**, then paste their translation into the **Welsh (Cymraeg)** section of the job and click **Publish**.
- Fill in **all** the Welsh fields or none. A half-finished Welsh version is ignored.

---

## 6. Changing or removing something

- **To fix a mistake:** open the entry, change it, and click **Publish** again. (Reviews: only to fix _our_ mistakes, like the wrong date or star count. Never change the review text.)
- **To take something off the website:** open it, click the **⋯** menu next to Publish, and choose **Unpublish**. It stays in the admin area as a draft.
- **To delete it completely:** **⋯** → **Delete**. This can't be undone.
- **Never change a job's Web address** after it's published.

## 7. Troubleshooting

| Problem                                                 | What to do                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **I published but it's not on the website**             | Wait 3 minutes and refresh the page. Check the entry says **Published**, not **Draft**. Still missing after 10 minutes? Tell [site owner]. |
| **"No documents of this type"**                         | The list is empty. Click the pencil icon to add the first one.                                                                             |
| **The Publish button is greyed out**                    | A required field is empty. Look for red warnings in the form.                                                                              |
| **The photo is cropped badly**                          | Open the photo field and drag the circle (hotspot) over the important part.                                                                |
| **The stars aren't showing under the homepage buttons** | They only appear at 10+ reviews averaging 4.5 or more (see section 4).                                                                     |
| **I can't log in**                                      | Ask [site owner] to check your invite.                                                                                                     |
